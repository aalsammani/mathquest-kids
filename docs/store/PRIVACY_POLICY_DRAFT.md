## The short version

MathQuest Kids does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following **only on your device**:
- a **nickname** you or your child may enter (optional; please do not use a full name);
- the chosen mascot, coins, and badges;
- learning progress: which skills were practiced, whether answers were correct, whether a hint or a second try was used, and dates of practice;
- settings such as sound, read-aloud, text size, and break reminders.

This information is stored in the browser's local storage (web version) or in the app's private storage (Android and iOS apps). **We never receive it.** It is not uploaded to any server, including cloud backup: on Android, the app's data is excluded from Google cloud backup.

## What we do not do

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, or device or advertising identifiers. We do not use cookies for tracking, third-party analytics, advertising, or social features. The app does not contain chat, links to other websites, or purchases.

## Read-aloud

If read-aloud is turned on, question text is passed to your device's built-in text-to-speech feature so it can be spoken. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

## Grown-up features

A parental gate protects the dashboard. From there, a parent can:
- **export** progress as a CSV or backup file (the file is saved or shared to a place **you** choose using your device's normal download or share options);
- **restore** a backup file;
- **delete** a child's profile or **erase all data**.

Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) also removes the stored data.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. Because we do not collect personal information from children, we do not need to ask for verifiable parental consent to collect it. If you believe information has been sent to us in error, contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL] and we will help.

## Security

Data stays inside the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Please use a device passcode.

## Changes

If we ever change how the app handles information (for example, by adding an online feature), we will update this policy and the app store privacy disclosures **before** the change takes effect, and we will ask for parental consent where the law requires it.
